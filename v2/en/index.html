a glass of water comes free!

A small family-run coffee bar a few minutes from St. Stephen's Cathedral. Viennese coffee served the traditional way. From five, the cups turn into glasses.

See the menu ★★★★★ 4.9 · 196 Google reviews

From earlytill late.

A day on the Postgasse.

  1. Thu–Sat from 9 amEspresso & paperThe day starts short and strong.
  2. Mon–Wed from 3:30 pmMelange & gugelhupfA proper Viennese afternoon.
  3. from 5 pmAperitivoCups out, glasses in.
  4. until 10 pmOne more glassThen it’s closing time.

How it works here

  • First come, first seated. No reservations.
  • Your glass of water comes without asking.
  • Inside at the bar or outside on the street terrace.
  • Dogs are welcome.
  • Cards are accepted.
  • For groups: rent the whole place.
